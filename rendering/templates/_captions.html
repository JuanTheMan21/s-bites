Before this, every word ever shown stayed on screen for the rest of the scene, so captions grew into an unreadable wall of text -- the exact bug this replaces. A cue's words are NOT inked in one at a time. An earlier version kept a per-word karaoke-style reveal inside each cue (each word dimmed until its own real `offset_ms`) -- found, after a real render, to still read as "appearing word by word" even though the wall-of-text bug was fixed; the whole point of a movie-style caption is that a line (or two) appears as one unit.
